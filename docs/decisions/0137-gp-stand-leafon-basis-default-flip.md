* **Status:** accepted
* **Date:** 2026-08-13
* **Line:** M (multi-cell coupled S+F+E; rung 3 of `EXECUTION_PLAN.md`)
* **Discharges:** ADR 0136 §7's pre-registered flip criterion. Conditions 1–2 were met when it was written; condition 3 (the measured blast radius) was run on 2026-08-13, came back at **23 assertions across eight files**, and is enumerated and closed here.
* **Cross-line:** the one line-S-owned casualty (`test/testitems/slow_level_anchor_tests.jl:181`) was raised as an integration point on 2026-08-13 and **line S replied GO, option (a)**, authorising M to re-pin it inside this commit (`lines/M/STATE.md`, inbound of 2026-08-13; `lines/S/STATE.md`).

* **Related:** ADR 0136 (which measured the flag), ADR 0135 (the compensating-error reading), ADR 0133 (the previous default flip and the control-arm trap), ADR 0059 (the `wscal_leafon` flip, the precedent for a cross-line GO), ADR 0051 (which registered the difference).

## 1. Decision

**`WaterParams.gp_stand_leafon_basis` defaults to `true`.** F_diff's multi-individual canopy path now
forms the stand conductance the way `gp_sum.c` does: each individual's potential conductance is built at
**full leaf cover** (`apar ∝ fpc`, no `phen`, and `gmin·fpc` likewise), the stand numerator is
`Σ gp_leafon·φ`, and the normalisation is the **plain** `Σ fpc`. F's previous expression folded `φ` into
the pass-1 `apar` and into `gmin` and divided by the phen-weighted `Σ fpc·φ`, which biases the stand
conductance high by ≈`1/φ̄` on every partial-leaf day and carries that bias into `demand`, `gc`, `gpd`,
`fac`, the solved λ and hence GPP.

Guardrail 4 is now served by the **opt-out**: `gp_stand_leafon_basis = false` reproduces the pre-ADR-0136
expression exactly, and every arm that means the old basis states it (they were written to state it
*before* this flip — ADR 0136 §6, and see §5).

`lambda_vm_gp` is **NOT** flipped and is not a flip candidate: it is equally faithful and makes agreement
**worse** at 5 of 5 cells, which is the compensating-error measurement (ADR 0136 §5). It stays opt-in as
the control for that search.

## 3. The blast radius, discharged

Of the 23 assertions:

**(a) 10 were VACUOUS, not wrong — `gpsum_basis_tests.jl`.** Its control `pbase` took the package default
by omission, so at the flip it became a second copy of the treatment arm and every comparison against it
went empty. Session 22 had already pinned both flags in all four arms; what remained here was re-pointing
the four guardrail-4 assertions at the new default and re-writing the default/arm equality so that it now
reads `default == pgs` **and** `default != pbase`. That pair is what will break at any *further* default
move — the mechanism that caught this one.

**(b) 6 were committed baselines and pins, re-recorded through their producing scripts, each with a
control arm reproducing the pre-flip numbers in the same run.**

* `test/testitems/references/hainich_canopy_baseline_2010.txt` (→ `multi_individual_tests.jl:190-194`),
  regenerated by `scripts/regen_fdiff_baselines.jl`, whose `gps = false` arm reproduced the previous
  `gpp_annual` to **every printed digit** (1237.4371145997352). `gpp_annual` **1237.437115 → 1143.375187**
  (−7.60 %), `transp_annual` −4.38 %, `evap_annual` +0.93 %, `rootmoist_mean` +0.38 %, `interc_annual`
  unmoved.
* `test/testitems/biome_coupled_tests.jl:330-336`, the five coupled biome signatures, re-measured by
  `scripts/biome_ensemble_pin_probe.jl`. The `GPSTAND=0` arm returned **all ten** committed numbers to
  every printed digit. GPP moves **−11.6 %** at `boreal_siberia`, −6.0 % Hainich, −2.8 % Iberia, −0.5 %
  Sahel, −0.5 % Amazon; LE −0.7 % to −0.03 % on the same ordering. The ordering is the mechanism's: the
  flag can only bind on partial-leaf days, so the most leaf-off cell moves most and the evergreen tropical
  cell least — the same ranking ADR 0136 pre-registered and confirmed.
* `sapwood_bg_tests.jl:122/131`, the two carbon-use-efficiency pins: **0.5118 → 0.4843** unseeded and
  **0.497 → 0.4679** seeded. The seeding step still costs the same ~0.016 of CUE, and the seeded value
  moves **toward** the C's own 0.46, not away.

**(c) 4 were fidelity BANDS, re-stated with the new measurement and never widened.**

* `decadal_validation_tests.jl:102` — the decadal GPP level, `[1.0, 1.12] → [0.92, 1.02]`, measured
  **0.964132**. Band width unchanged (0.10) with the measurement mid-band. ⚠ **Read the direction
  honestly: F now sits ~3.6 % BELOW the C over the decade rather than ~4 % above — on this statistic the
  flip did not shrink the error, it crossed zero.**
* `decadal_validation_tests.jl:103` and `wscal_leafon_tests.jl:218` were written as `all(...)`
  assertions, which print nothing but `false` when they fail; both are now asserted on the extremum, so
  the next basis change reads its own magnitude off the first run instead of paying for a second job.
* `wscal_leafon_tests.jl:93` — `sens_df`, how much the *pre-ADR-0051* realized-ratio water scalar depends
  on leaf display: `> 0.4 → > 0.15`, measured **0.2059**. This assertion is about the legacy arm's
  pathology, and the flip genuinely reduces it: part of that arm's phen sensitivity entered through
  `gp_stand`'s phen weighting, which the C's basis removes. The contrast assertion `sens_df > 10·sens_lo`
  and the whole `wscal_leafon` gate stayed green.

**⚠ (c′) ONE OF THOSE FOUR IS A SCIENCE FINDING AND NOT A RE-PIN — `wscal_leafon_tests.jl:218`.** The
assertion said the pre-ADR-0051 realized-ratio water-stress feature misses the demo artifact's trained
band by **more than five band widths**; the threshold `hi + 5·(hi − lo)` = **0.2589** has to drop to plain
`hi`. Measured `min(ws_df)` = **0.0561** against `hi` = 0.043155, `lo` = 0.0 and a C truth of 0.0014 —
still out of band, but by **~0.30 band widths instead of >5**. Mechanism: that arm's stress is
`1 − supply/demand`, `demand` is proportional to the stand conductance, and F's conductance was inflated
by ≈`1/φ̄`. **So most of what ADR 0034 §1 and ADR 0051 measured as the old definition's error was F's own
`gp_sum` basis, not the `wscal` definition.** Which definition is faithful is unchanged and the gate still
separates the two arms (the C-faithful one is inside the band, this one is not) — but *"many band widths
out"* is a statement on the pre-0137 basis and must not be re-quoted on this one. `CLAUDE.md` §3's
`wscal` bullet carries the same 0.305 figure and now says so.

**(d) 3 were a coupling magnitude that GENUINELY GREW — `grass_structure_tests.jl:256/257`, rtol
0.02 → 0.05.** Under F's old basis a change in the grass's leaf display moved the `gp_stand` numerator and
denominator together and largely cancelled; the C's basis divides by the **plain** `Σ fpc`, so the same
change passes into the stand conductance undamped. The tree↔grass competition in a mixed patch is
therefore stronger, not noisier: worst case 4.9 % (`leaf_c` 366.09 → 348.21), then 4.0 % and 2.9 %, all
under 2 % before. This is a mechanism, so it is re-stated with the mechanism rather than absorbed.

**(e) 1 was line-S-owned — `slow_level_anchor_tests.jl:181`, `ret_025 > 0.7` → `> 0.55`, measured
0.618996.** S's reasoning for option (a), recorded so it is not re-derived: yr 25 is the horizon the
file's own comment flags in capitals as non-monotone and as *"the worst point of that transient"*, while
ADR 0103's claim is the **long-horizon** separation (unanchored 1.036 vs anchored 0.051 at yr 150, ~20×),
and **all four contrast assertions stayed green** under the flip, so "the anchor strictly beats
no-anchor" is undamaged.

⚠ **UNMEASURED, and S asked for it to be said rather than implied: the yr-150 UNANCHORED retention under
the flip.** The suite reports yr 5 and yr 25 only, because those are what the file asserts; the 150-yr
horizon needs its own job. If it had fallen materially (below ~0.8 against 1.036 today) then F really
would be forgetting its own initial condition and option (b) would have been live. Line S has queued an
explicit long-horizon separation assertion in its own file; that is S's work, not M's.

## 4. Evidence that this is a measured basis change and not a re-record

Three independent checks, all in the runs that produced the new numbers:

1. **Every regenerated artifact has a control arm that reproduced the pre-flip value in the same run** —
   the canopy baseline's `gpp_annual` to every printed digit, and all ten coupled pins to every printed
   digit. A knob added *after* a flip can reproduce nothing, which is why these opt-outs were written
   before it (ADR 0133 §6).
2. **The single-individual baselines did not move at all.** `hainich_fdiff_baseline_2010.txt` and
   `hainich_ml_baseline_2010.txt` reproduce exactly, which is the flag's documented scope (`daily_step_canopy`
   only) confirmed as a measurement rather than a claim.
3. **The direction is one-signed everywhere and ordered by leaf-off incidence** — five coupled cells, the
   canopy baseline, the decadal rollout and the unit-level direction loop all move GPP down, most where
   the canopy spends most time below full leaf.

⚠ **One honest residue.** The canopy baseline's control arm reproduces the previous `gpp_annual` exactly
but the four WATER rows only to ≤ **5.1e-4** relative (interc worst, then evap 1.6e-4, transp 9.7e-5,
rootmoist 4.2e-7). That drift is **pre-existing and provably not this flip**: all three arms in job 1788021
agree on `interc_annual` bitwise. It is the same sub-tolerance accumulation the ADR-0133 note recorded at
≤ 1.6e-4 and deliberately left in place; this regeneration absorbs it, so the next drift measurement starts
from zero instead of from an unlabelled offset.

## 5. Consequences

* **The labels shifted in every probe that pins the flag.** `Pgbgg` is now the **pre-flip** configuration
  and `Pgbggs` the shipping one; `biome_sapwood_bg_probe.jl`'s 30 committed decomposition rows, and the
  published panels of `biome_canopy_growth_probe.jl` / `biome_resilience_probe.jl` /
  `biome_slow_oracle_probe.jl`, are all on a basis the package no longer runs by default. Their headers
  now say so. Re-measuring any of them on the new default is a deliberate new arm.
* **The differentiated (trainer) path inherits the flip.** `rollout_canopy_years_gpp` reads `p.water` with
  no reconstruction, so the AD trainer now runs the leaf-on basis, as it already inherited
  `tree_demand_gate = true` at the soft sharpness (ADR 0133). Any trainer arm that means "the pre-0137
  path" must say so explicitly.
* **`GPP_F/GPP_C` remains a LOWER bound on F's tree-photosynthesis error** (ADR 0135 §5, ADR 0136 §3).
  This flip removes one of the terms that inflated F's GPP, and the decadal level crossing from +4 % to
  −3.6 % is consistent with a compensating error still being present rather than with the error being
  closed. The head of the photosynthesis queue is unchanged.
* **The remaining shortlist item is (c), the phenology trajectory**, scoped to two concrete
  simplifications — the per-stem `minwscal` inflection and the air-temperature-driven soil-temperature
  gate. Item (a) closed with ADR 0136, item (b) with ADR 0138.

## 6. What would overturn this

* The yr-150 unanchored retention under the flip falling below ~0.8 (§3e) — that would mean F's own
  dynamics now forget the initial density, which is part of what the level anchor exists to do, and would
  re-open line S's option (b).
* A cell or configuration where the flag makes GPP *rise*. The mechanism forbids it (the C's basis can
  only lower the stand conductance on a partial-leaf day, and is an exact identity at `φ ≡ 1`), so an
  observation of that would mean the port is wrong rather than the parameterisation.
